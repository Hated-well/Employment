Our Values

The principles that guide how we work together and build CoreStory.

Build Deeply

We build for the long term. We make thoughtful decisions that compound — in our code, our product, and our culture.

Move With Urgency

We act fast and ship daily. Progress compounds — we'd rather learn quickly from reality than deliberate endlessly.

Communicate Honestly

We believe in clear, direct, kind communication. No politics, no ego — just good people working on hard problems together.

Customer Obsessed
